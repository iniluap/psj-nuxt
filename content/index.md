Since 2017, I've built interfaces used by millions of people daily, with a&nbsp;focus
on making the web accessible to everyone.

In 2020, I&nbsp;joined
[Instapage](https://www.instapage.com/){.inline-link}, an enterprise Angular
application in the marketing tech space, as one of two frontend engineers. From 2022
to 2026, I&nbsp;was a&nbsp;software engineer at
[GitLab](https://about.gitlab.com/){.inline-link}, where I&nbsp;contributed to the
<strong>Source Code Management</strong> group and led the
<strong>Product Accessibility Working Group</strong>.

My work spans the full development lifecycle, from early ideation and design
collaboration to implementation, testing, and measuring impact in production.
I&nbsp;specialise in <strong>Vue.js</strong>, <strong>digital accessibility</strong>,
and <strong>complex legacy codebases</strong>. I'm passionate about well-organised
code, inclusive design, and making sure every feature I&nbsp;build works seamlessly
with assistive technologies.

Before tech, I&nbsp;studied classical music and English Studies with a&nbsp;focus on
sociolinguistics, and taught English as a&nbsp;Second Language. Along the way, I met
and worked with people with physical and learning disabilities: from children
realising their full potential thanks to educational accommodations, to
a&nbsp;deaf percussionist and a&nbsp;one-handed cellist performing specially composed
pieces with a&nbsp;symphonic orchestra
([see the performance on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bCq7GUAjE68){.inline-link}).
They taught me that it's social barriers and exclusion that limit people's abilities,
not differences in how our bodies work.

As a&nbsp;frontend engineer, I strive to make online experiences available to
everyone. I believe developers have an obligation not to add new barriers to the
digital world, which can take many people to places they cannot reach physically.
